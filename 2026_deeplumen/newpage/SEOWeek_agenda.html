Key Takeaways

The 8 things your brand can't ignore.

From 40+ sessions, these are the insights with the most direct impact on how DTC brands acquire customers and grow revenue in 2026.

How AI Finds You

When someone asks ChatGPT "best moisturizer for dry skin," there's a whole process behind which brands get mentioned — and you can influence each step

AI answers aren't random. They're assembled through a pipeline: find content, score it, rank it, cite it. Brands that understand this pipeline get recommended more.

Michael King PPT p.28, p.48
Brand Visibility

85% of what AI says about your brand comes from reviews, press, and third-party sites — not your own website

You don't control your AI narrative. If your brand isn't showing up consistently on review sites, media, and forums, AI will either ignore you or get your story wrong.

Ross Simmonds PPT p.48, p.83
How AI Finds You

Connecting your product data properly makes AI answers 29% more accurate about your brand

Adding schema markup alone isn't enough. Brands that connect their product info, brand pages, and review data into a clear web of information get dramatically better AI representation.

Andrea Volpini PPT p.15
AI Shopping Agents

AI agents are already browsing, comparing prices, and buying on behalf of consumers — your product page needs to work for robots too

Google, OpenAI, and others are building AI shopping agents. If your site can't be read and validated by these agents, you're invisible to a growing share of buyers.

Crystal Carter PPT p.21-28
Brand Visibility

Don't abandon Google SEO for AI hype — SEO still drives measurable revenue, AI visibility doesn't yet

Before chasing AI rankings, check how accurately AI describes your brand today. If it's getting your products or positioning wrong, fix that first.

Wil Reynolds PPT p.77-78
Shopper Behavior

Consumers are increasingly asking AI "what should I buy?" instead of browsing — your content needs to be the answer AI picks

The purchase funnel is fracturing. Shoppers delegate decisions to AI, and AI needs clear, structured, trustworthy content to confidently recommend your brand.

Azeem Ahmad PPT
Content That Sells

The best product pages now work three ways at once: they rank on Google, get cited by AI, and convert visitors

Don't build separate strategies for SEO, AI visibility, and conversion. The winning brands create single pages that perform across all three channels.

Amanda Milligan PPT
Marketing ROI

Stop measuring SEO by traffic — measure it by revenue impact, and you'll get budget approved faster

"$12M in pipeline at risk" gets a CEO's attention. Reframing your marketing spend around financial outcomes is how modern DTC brands win resources.

Dale Bertrand PPT
